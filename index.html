01 · Profile

Personal Profile

Education and research motivation

I am Jinmian Li, an undergraduate in Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering Technology at Shenzhen Polytechnic University, where I have been studying since 2024.

I am interested in robots with strong generalization, reliability, and learning capabilities. My goal is to help robots acquire skills from minimal expert data and carry out tasks through natural-language instructions or demonstrations.

My coursework includes mathematics, mechanics of materials, mechanical manufacturing, embedded systems, industrial robotics, and intelligent manufacturing equipment.

Research Directions

From 3D representations and manufacturing to efficient robot learning

01 · 3D Representation

3D Gaussian Splatting

Exploring 3DGS for high-precision model generation for 3D printing and world model construction.

02 · Manufacturing

Additive & Subtractive Manufacturing

Exploring the integration of additive manufacturing technologies and the development and application of hybrid additive and subtractive processes.

03 · Embodied Intelligence

Vision-Language-Action & World Models

Investigating improvements to vision-language-action (VLA) models and world models (WM), and their applications in embodied systems.

04 · Robot Learning

Data-Efficient Learning

Studying how robots can acquire skills and achieve operational goals with very little expert data or without expert demonstrations.
