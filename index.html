About

Hi, my name is Jinmian Li. I am a Research Assistant / Independent Researcher at Shenzhen Polytechnic University. My research explores how intelligent design and robotic systems can make fabrication more adaptive, functional, and responsive to people and the physical world.

I am particularly interested in integrating design-for-manufacturing with robotic perception and action, bridging digital models, manufacturing processes, and embodied interaction. A fuller biography, education history, awards, and experience will be added from my curriculum vitae.
