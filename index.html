01 · Profile

Personal Profile

Background, current position, and research motivation

I am Jinmian Li, a Research Assistant / Independent Researcher at Shenzhen Polytechnic University. My research lies at the intersection of advanced manufacturing and intelligent robotic systems.

I am interested in integrating design-for-manufacturing with robotic perception, planning, and action, with the goal of enabling fabrication systems that are more adaptive, functional, and responsive to real-world needs.
