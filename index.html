About

I am a Research Assistant / Independent Researcher at Shenzhen Polytechnic University. My research interests lie at the intersection of additive manufacturing and design, robotics, and embodied intelligence. I am particularly interested in integrating design-for-manufacturing with intelligent robotic systems to enable more adaptive, functional, and human-centered fabrication and interaction.

Research Interests
